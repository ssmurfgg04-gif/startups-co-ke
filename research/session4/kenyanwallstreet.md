## 5. Copywriting patterns to steal (analysis grounded in the above URLs)
1. Number-first specificity: "Declines 13.7% to KSh 417.7 Million"; "Two years at KSh129"; "Inflation is up 6.8% and milk is a major culprit" (odd-detail twist).
2. Named-actor + verb-of-motion: "KQ's minority shareholders to face further dilution"; "Why Kenyan workers are cashing out their pensions".
3. Colloquial-quote headlines: "'The IMF can wait' - CBK Governor Thugge".
4. Duty framing: "Kenya's #1 newsletter among business leaders & policy makers" — flattery-as-positioning repeated on every issue.
5. Habit anchors: fixed times (Mon 9am EAT + weekday 7pm EAT) + same host name ("I'm Brian…") + channel set (email/LinkedIn/Telegram/WhatsApp/X) so one story re-reaches users 5+ ways.
6. Interactive hook: NSE Investment Challenge converts readers into players (cash prizes, students & young professionals target).
