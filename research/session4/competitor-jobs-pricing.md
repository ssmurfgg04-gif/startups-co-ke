# Job-posting pricing & trust in Kenya — competitor scan
_Compiled 2026-10-15 (Task 4-research). All prices are exactly as published on the cited page (KES where stated). Prices behind login/quote walls are marked "not public"._

## 1. Paid posting — verified prices

- **Pay-as-you-go: KSh 2,900 per job post.** Verbatim inclusions: "Your job is active for 3 months or you set your choice deadline. Your job is featured at a top spot on our website for 3 weeks. Increased candidate reach via website, email, partner websites and social media. Receive applications via your email, link out to your ATS or collect applications on MyJobMag. Use our ATS and set pre-qualifying custom screening questions. AI Match scores every applicant's CV… Download all or shortlisted CVs." — https://www.myjobmag.co.ke/employers
- **Unlimited plan: KSh 14,000 / month** — "Post unlimited jobs… Includes 10 candidate search credits." — https://www.myjobmag.co.ke/employers
- **Shortlisting service: KSh 24,000 per role** — "We will send you 8 - 10 candidates that match your job description in 5 business days." (delivered within 3–5 business days) — https://www.myjobmag.co.ke/employers
- **Free tier: KSh 0** — "Free Job Posting KSh 0 per job role. Enjoy basic features… No support available." — https://www.myjobmag.co.ke/employers
- Feature framing used: "x3 visibility", "AI CV Ranking", "SCREENING QUESTIONS", "ONLINE ASSESSMENT" — https://www.myjobmag.co.ke/employers ; free posting also promoted on Facebook — https://www.facebook.com/myjobmag.co.ke/posts/1387065216902618

## 3. What free channels lack (observed gaps, sourced)
- **No employer verification / scam noise**: fee scams spread through exactly these free channels (Fuzu Inuka 8 warning signs incl. "Everything happens on WhatsApp only") — https://inuka.fuzu.com/spot-a-fake-job ; Africa Check on fake KSh1,000/day Facebook ads — https://africacheck.org/fact-checks/meta-programme-fact-checks/scam-alert-beware-facebook-posts-advertising-ksh1000-daily
- **No structure**: hashtag/group posts have no fields (no salary band, no deadline, no filters) — cf. ad structure evidence — https://www.theafricareport.com/13844/kenya-can-a-hashtag-save-the-job-market
- **No application pipeline**: groups route to WhatsApp DMs; boards like MyJobMag sell ATS + AI ranking + screening questions as the paid delta — https://www.myjobmag.co.ke/employers
- **Trust choreography legit boards display**: "never pay to apply/interview" pledges (https://www.careerpointkenya.co.ke/upload-your-cv , https://www.myjobsinkenya.com), "verified employers" (https://ke.linkedin.com/company/brightermonday-com), pre-review of every role + no-fee pledge (https://inuka.fuzu.com/spot-a-fake-job).

## 4. Pricing takeaways for a startup-jobs board
- Published Kenyan anchor points: **KSh 0** (BrighterMonday basic, Fuzu Free Forever, CareerPoint, MyJobMag basic), **KSh 2,900** (MyJobMag single post), **KSh 6,000–30,000** (Fuzu Starter tiers), **KSh 14,000/mo** (MyJobMag unlimited), **KSh 24,000/role** (shortlisting service). — URLs in §1
- BrighterMonday moved its entry product to free in 2025 — the "KES 5,000-valued basic listing" language is itself a pricing anchor — https://www.brightermonday.co.ke/discover/brighter-mondays-are-hire-right-with-our-freelisting-offer
- Nobody publishes a startup-niche job board price; the paid delta everyone sells is screening/ATS/visibility, not the listing itself.
