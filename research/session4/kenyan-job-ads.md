# How Kenyan job ads actually present themselves
_Compiled 2026-10-15 (Task 4-research). Every claim has a source URL. Items that could not be verified are marked UNVERIFIED._

## 1. Typical structure of a Kenyan job advert
- Canonical skeleton observed across live Kenyan ads: **Job title → Organisation blurb → Duties/Responsibilities → Qualifications/Requirements → "How to Apply" (email + deadline)**.
- "How to Apply" is almost always a CV-to-email instruction. Verbatim examples:
  - "To apply Send Your CV to: Email : palmmoraineschool@gmail.com" — Corporate Staffing Kenya job listing — https://www.corporatestaffing.co.ke/jobs
  - "To apply for the above position, please send your CV by email, listing three references including your last direct supervisor and salary expectation to…" — Kenya job-opportunities digest — https://www.scribd.com/document/88770643/Job-Opportunities-Kenya-April-10
  - "To apply send your CV to the email address on the ad." — Brites Management (recruiter) Facebook ad — https://www.facebook.com/Britesmanagement/posts/966809732779944
- Structured-field format used by NGOs/employers (note the literal "Salary:" and "Job Location:" fields): "Employment Type: Contractual. Salary: Negotiable. Job Location: Nairobi, Kenya… If you feel you are the right match for the above-mentioned position, please…" — BRAC Finance Controller, Nairobi — https://www.devex.com/jobs/finance-controller-brac-international-holdings-b-v-1084663 and same template at https://millionexperts.com/jobs/Job-announcement/Manager-IT
- Fully-disclosed-salary variant: "Salary: Monthly Gross Salary: KSH 80,000 + discretionary year-end bonus (Approx 618 USD). Location: Nairobi, Kenya. Career Level: Lower Mid-Level" — Summit Recruitment — https://summitrecruitment-search.com/career-levels/lower-mid-level
- Deadlines: Kenyan recruitment firms publicise "application deadline" as a hard gate (e.g., "Application deadline is May 15, 2025" — Standard Group Territory Executives ad) — https://www.facebook.com/standardkenya/posts/1133988902104245
- Referees are frequently demanded in the ad itself ("listing three references including your last direct supervisor" above). Board summary: "Post A Free Job Advert Professional CV Writing… " shows boards package ads with CV services — https://www.careerpointkenya.co.ke
- Physical drop-off survives: "I take my hardcopy CV. And drop…" — r/Kenya on how Kenyans apply while tarmacking — https://www.reddit.com/r/Kenya/comments/54xlez/looking_for_a_job

## 3. Application conventions in Kenya
- The document is a **CV**, never "résumé"; 2–3 pages, no photo, ends with named referees: "A standard Kenyan CV runs two to three pages with no photo, closes with two to three named referees." — Qarera Kenya CV guide — https://www.qarera.com/resume-templates/kenya
- Counter-opinion circulating among Kenyan HR: "Referees should NOT be included on a CV except when expressly requested." — Shem Ngatia, LinkedIn — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/shem-ngatia-77b31a112_should-referees-be-included-in-your-cv-activity-7419282307431321600-jy0n
- Kenyan ads still ask for documents global boards dropped: "Recent Kenyan job advertisements can explicitly ask candidates for age, marital status, copies of ID documents, referees or a passport [photo]." — Vezill CV guide (Kenya) — https://vezill.com/blogs/how-to-write-a-strong-cv-for-jobs-in-kenya
- **WhatsApp apply** is a real Kenyan channel, alongside email: "Send Your Application via WhatsApp. Or email: careers@amazingparties.co.ke" — Amazing Theme Party Planners (Nairobi) careers page — https://amazingparties.co.ke/careers ; "Send your application via WhatsApp by attaching…" — AfricaCareers listing — https://www.africareers.net/jobs/lady-host-job-at-zouk-lounge
- **LinkedIn Easy Apply** operates in Kenya (1,000+ tech jobs on LinkedIn KE; Easy Apply = 1-click with LinkedIn profile, cannot be edited after submit) — https://ke.linkedin.com/jobs/tech-jobs and https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a512388
- Certificate attachments: Kenyan ads requesting "copies of ID documents" etc. (Vezill, above) reflect the attach-certificates norm. Specific "attach KCSE certificate" wording: UNVERIFIED in this pass.
- Email-app etiquette is coached locally ("Remember to write a professional email with subject line…" — Kenyan cruise-jobs TikTok, weak source) — https://www.tiktok.com (via search snippet; UNVERIFIED page)

## 4. Scam patterns vs. trust signals (critical for us)
- The fee scam is the dominant pattern. Fuzu's Inuka anti-scam page (Kenya) is the best single source; verbatim: "**The golden rule: a real job never asks you to pay. Ever.** … 1 They ask you to pay — Registration fee, training fee, medical fee, uniform fee, 'refundable' deposit… 7 They ask for your PIN, OTP or documents upfront — No employer needs your M-Pesa PIN…" — https://inuka.fuzu.com/spot-a-fake-job
- Same source names the channel risk: "4 Everything happens on WhatsApp only — An offer that exists only inside a chat thread usually isn't real." — https://inuka.fuzu.com/spot-a-fake-job
- Telecom-level warning: "Fraudsters send texts or make calls with fake job opportunities that require you to pay a fee." — Safaricom Fraud Awareness — https://www.safaricom.co.ke/fraud-awareness/scams-extortion
- Fake-day-rate ads on Facebook: "Job ads offering KSh1,000 a day for light manual [work]… display several red flags" — Africa Check — https://africacheck.org/fact-checks/meta-programme-fact-checks/scam-alert-beware-facebook-posts-advertising-ksh1000-daily
- Big-employer public disclaimers (trust-signal playbook): "A genuine employer will never ask you to pay before offering you a job." — Airkenya — https://www.facebook.com/Airkenya/posts/1825072242474976 ; "No recruitment fees required" — Standard Group — https://www.facebook.com/standardkenya/posts/1133988902104245 ; "KRA only posts job vacancies on our official website" — KRA Care — https://www.facebook.com/KRACare/videos/476682037736704
- Community-side warning: "Legitimate employers never demand payment for training, uniforms, or 'registration fees.'" — r/Kenya job-scam thread — https://www.reddit.com/r/Kenya/comments/1kai0al/job_scam_i_almost_fell_for_a_job_scam_please_be
- Board-side trust signals to copy:

Employers also sign the **Fair Work Pledge** — a public commitment to fair treatment, **pay transparency** and a strict no-fee policy." — https://inuka.fuzu.com/spot-a-fake-job
