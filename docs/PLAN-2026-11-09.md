# Plan - 2026-11-09 Monday system

Date: 2026-11-09 EAT, desk startups.co.ke

## What we studied

- Lenny's Newsletter: one idea, short paragraphs, subject under 42 chars, single CTA, no news dump. Read in 90 seconds.
- Platformer: one argument per issue, why it matters in plain language, sources at end, no bullet spam.
- Disrupt Africa Pulse: headline is always who plus how much plus who led. Amount undisclosed is stated, never invented.
- Kenyan Wall Street Daily Brief: Kenyan money first, KSh precision, what it means for builders today.
- WeeTracker and TechCabal: Africa big picture with Kenya cut.

Jobs pattern (Wellfound, LinkedIn): role title, company one-liner, location, pay band, apply link. No salary invented. That is our ATS pull plus employer flow.
Events pattern (Luma, GDG, Tech Events Kenya): date, venue, tracks, organizer, price, countdown. Our hackathons page already does this. Gap is auto fetch.
