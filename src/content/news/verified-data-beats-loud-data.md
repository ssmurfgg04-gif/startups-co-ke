Every ecosystem directory eventually faces the same temptation: pad the numbers. A thousand listings looks stronger than four hundred. Investors skim, journalists copy, and the inflated index becomes the story everyone tells about the market. We think this is quietly corrosive, and it's why this platform is built around statuses instead of volume.

Our design fiction — and we label it as such everywhere — is simple: every company is either Verified (registry plus team checks) or Unverified (self-reported, pending). Every funding figure is Confirmed, Reported or Estimated, with a named source type. None of this is real data yet; all of it is real discipline. The moment you can mark a figure "Estimated," you have admitted the index is a living thing, and readers can weigh it honestly.

The deeper argument is that ecosystems mature through their information infrastructure. Kenya's startup economy already produces enough noise. What it lacks is a shared, honest ledger — one where "we don't know yet" is a first-class status. Loud data flatters the ecosystem. Verified data strengthens it. We choose the second.
