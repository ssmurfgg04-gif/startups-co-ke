When HoneyCoin closed its $4.9M seed in August 2025, the round's most telling detail was not the size — it was the geography. A Nairobi startup, born in Antler's residency on Riverside Drive, building hybrid fiat-stablecoin payment rails for African and global markets, with Flourish Ventures (the fintech-focused impact investor) participating.

Stablecoins solve a specific Kenyan problem: cross-border settlement is slow and expensive, while domestic rails — M-PESA above all — are excellent. The new stack being built by HoneyCoin and peers keeps the excellent domestic layer and replaces the broken international one: stablecoin treasury for businesses, fiat on/off-ramps at the edges, KYC/AML in the middle.

Regulation is the swing factor. Kenya's fintech reforms reported by TechCabal in October 2026 promise clarity but could delay investment in the near term; the CBDC conversation has moved from whether to how. For founders, the honest read is that this layer will be built — the open question is whether it is built by Kenyan companies or for Kenyan customers by others.
