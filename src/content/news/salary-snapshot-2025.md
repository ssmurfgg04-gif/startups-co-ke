Compensation moved selectively this year (sample data, 61 contributing startups). Backend remained the hottest function: the 90th percentile crossed KSh 600K/month for staff-level engineers, driven by three Series A rounds competing for the same distributed-systems experience. Data and ML tracked close behind, with remote-sensing specialists commanding a premium few companies had budgeted.

The quiet story is statutory. The SHIF transition added real cost to every payroll — and startups responded in kind: 9% of contributing companies shifted a slice of comp into performance bonuses, and a handful introduced "compliance-neutral" benefit packages. Take-home became a bigger part of the conversation, and candidates started asking payroll-savvy questions in finals.

Design and growth tell the opposite story: bands held flat or slipped slightly in real terms, with senior designers increasingly contracting rather than going full-time. For founders, the actionable summary: budget backend hires at 2026 rates immediately, hold product bands but sweeten equity, and expect every senior candidate to arrive with a spreadsheet.

*All figures are illustrative samples describing no real employers.*
