The quarter closed with $38M tracked across 11 rounds — our strongest print since we began keeping this index, and the third consecutive quarter of growth (sample figures). But the headline number hides a more interesting story about composition. The median round is now $2.4M, up from $1.6M a year ago, while the count of sub-$500K cheques has fallen by a third. Capital is consolidating into fewer, more confident hands.

Three dynamics explain it. First, growth-stage investors are doubling down on companies that survived the 2024 squeeze: PesaFlow and Zuka Energy together account for nearly a quarter of the quarter's volume. Second, climate capital keeps multiplying its instrument set — grants, revenue-share, and equity now appear in the same cap tables. Third, the seed gap hasn't closed; it has moved. Angels and syndicates are writing cheques that micro-funds used to, and institutional seed funds are waiting longer before leading.

What should founders take from this? The bar for a "clean" Series A has risen: founders raising in 2026 are being asked for 18+ months of runway, credible cohort data, and a named expansion market. The companies closing rounds fastest all had one thing in common — a single metric they could defend for twenty minutes without a deck.
